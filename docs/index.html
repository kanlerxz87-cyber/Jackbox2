What is known

  • Asset type (scenario): Jack in the Box property with a NNN lease. User-provided, unverified
  • Location: Central Los Angeles, area-level only. Exact parcel withheld for privacy. Privacy control
  • Lease expiration window: 2030–2035. The exact expiration date, options, rent escalations, assignment clauses, and termination rights are unknown. Unverified
  • Decision question: Whether to sell before lease expiration or renew/restructure the lease, balancing wealth preservation, growth opportunity, and manageable risk. Scenario
  • Public context available: Official sources can inform area demographics, economy, planning, hazards, and tax rules. They cannot replace parcel-specific lease, rent roll, title, environmental, insurance, or tax-basis records. Scope boundary

What cannot be concluded now

  • No sell/renew winner: A responsible recommendation requires verified lease economics, actual NOI, tenant credit/sales context where legally available, tax basis, debt, capex, and market quotes. Decision gate
  • No parcel hazard determination: Regional hazard context is not a parcel-level determination. FEMA flood, CalFire FHSZ/LRA status, USGS seismic design/ground motion, insurance, and engineering must be checked for the exact parcel privately. Missing parcel data
  • No after-tax number: Capital gains, depreciation recapture, state tax, NIIT, installment/1031 eligibility, and net proceeds depend on verified owner/tax records. Missing tax inputs
  • No valuation: Short-lease discount, renewal probability, re-tenant/redevelopment cost, and exit liquidity require current market evidence and property documents. Missing market inputs
