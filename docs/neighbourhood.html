Why each matters for a drive-thru QSR

  • Planning: zoning, drive-thru rules, housing growth → traffic and labour pool.
  • Traffic: ADT, turning restrictions, transit → drive-thru throughput.
  • Schools: enrollment stability → household stability (context, not direct sales driver).
  • Competitors: same-store pressure; Jack system sales already negative (see Tract Trends).
