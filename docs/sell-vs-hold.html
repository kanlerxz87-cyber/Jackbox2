Rule: This page compares cases side-by-side. It does not recommend sell or hold. Any recommendation requires verified lease, basis, tract, hazard and pricing inputs (see Decision Gates).

Sell case (after-tax)

  • Net sale − selling costs − federal tax (cap gain + §1250 recapture) − CA tax (ordinary) − NIIT (if any) = after-tax proceeds.
  • 1031 exchange defers tax (does not eliminate) — qualification, recognized gain and deadlines must be checked with counsel and a qualified intermediary.
  • A shorter lease may affect pricing; the direction and amount are not verified (see Short-Lease Discount).

Hold / renewal case

  • NNN rent (verify bumps/options) − non-recoverable capex − vacancy/credit risk = NOI.
  • Renewal value depends on tenant coverage, remaining term, and Jack system trend (negative — see Tract Trends).
  • Hold PV must include tax on cash flow and exit cap — calculator requires adviser-estimated after-tax cash flows and exit proceeds.

Negotiated renewal case (what to ask for)

  • Longer term + options, fair-market bumps, percentage-rent floor, tenant financials / guaranty, estoppel, capex responsibilities.
  • Do not accept a renewal that leaves you with a short lease at expiry — price the discount explicitly.
