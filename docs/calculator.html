This calculator is optional; no owner input is needed to view the site. Values, rates and tax estimates are assumptions, not verified parcel data. The hold schedule is simplified and has not been back-tested. Do not make a decision based on its result.

Sale now

Ask a CPA for the incremental federal, California and applicable other tax on the sale, not a flat rate applied to all gains. Include debt payoff separately.




Hold / renewal

Enter a single year-one after-tax equity cash flow, including rent, expenses, debt service, capex and tax. Apply one assumed growth rate each year; this cannot represent separate vacancy or repair timing. Net exit proceeds must already deduct exit costs, taxes and debt payoff. Choose assumptions and stress tests with advisers.
