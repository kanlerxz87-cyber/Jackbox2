Public site boundaries

This is a public, static website, not an access-controlled workspace. Do not publish address, APN, lease, owner identity, financial details, or small-area findings that could reveal identity. Keep all parcel and owner records offline in an access-controlled workspace managed by authorized advisers. No such workspace is created or verified by this site.

Calculator

The calculator script has no analytics, storage or network calls; blank inputs and results remain in page memory until the tab is closed. Browser, operating system, extensions, screen sharing and device security are outside its control. Download the static site and use it offline on a trusted device for sensitive work. Clicking external source links contacts third-party sites and may disclose ordinary network metadata to them. Do not enter an address into this site.

Aggregate disclosure

Even small-area aggregates and date combinations can identify an asset. Review all proposed public additions for re-identification before publication.
