Stress-Test Rule (Required)

Required combined stress test. Do not evaluate hazards independently. Stress loss of rent + repairs/capex + insurance deductible/exclusion + exit liquidity/financing constraints together. A scenario that is survivable on one dimension may be unacceptable when combined.
