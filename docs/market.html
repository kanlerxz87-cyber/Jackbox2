Observations vs. Assumptions

Required distinction for decision integrity.

Observations (source-backed)

  • Official sources exist for county/tract demographics, labor, GDP, hazards, planning, and tax rules. Source Registry
  • FEMA NFHL is the official flood hazard database for NFIP flood hazard determinations where effective data exists. FEMA NFHL
  • CAL FIRE/OSFM provides official Fire Hazard Severity Zone maps and guidance. OSFM FHSZ
  • IRS Section 1031 applies to qualifying real property like-kind exchanges; rules and deadlines are specific. IRS 1031

Assumptions (not yet verified)

  • Exact lease expiration date within 2030–2035 window. Unverified
  • Tenant credit quality, store performance, rent coverage, and renewal intent. Unverified
  • Property condition, environmental status, title encumbrances, and insurability. Unverified
  • Market cap rate, buyer pool depth, and short-lease discount for this exact asset. Unverified

Area-level context is not parcel-specific investment advice. Verify all values on official sources before use.
