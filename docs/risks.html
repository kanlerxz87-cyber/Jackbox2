Stress test (do together, not separately)

Combine: assumed rent loss + major repairs + exit-price reduction. If hold PV collapses under stress, sell/renewal case must include a risk premium or exit. Have an adviser prepare a year-by-year stress schedule; the simplified calculator cannot model the timing of those shocks.

Best course (conditional)

Preserve wealth first: do not commit until gates pass. If verified coverage is strong and renewal extends term at fair rent, hold/renewal can preserve income; if coverage is weak, hazards are high, or short-lease discount is severe, compare the fully costed sale and renewal scenarios with advisers; those risks alone do not establish which choice preserves more capital. The site does not pick — gates decide.
