Minimum Evidence Package Before Decision

All items required for a reasonable sell/renew decision.

  • Executed lease abstract + amendments + notice calendar. Missing
  • Current rent roll, NOI, expense pass-throughs, capex history. Missing
  • At least two independent market value opinions with short-lease discount assumptions. Missing
  • CPA after-tax model for sell now, renew/hold, and 1031 scenarios. Missing
  • Parcel hazard screen (flood/fire/seismic/environmental) and insurance quotes. Missing
  • Title/legal/zoning review. Missing
  • Documented owner objectives, liquidity needs, and risk tolerance. Missing

This Q&A is a decision-readiness checklist, not a recommendation.
