Joint stress test

Once private inputs are verified, model earthquake, wildfire, flood and economic disruption individually and in combinations. Include simultaneous lost rent, deductible and uninsured repair costs, longer vacancy, higher premiums, tighter credit and a weaker exit price. Do not assign probabilities or loss amounts without defensible evidence; a severe scenario is not a prediction.
