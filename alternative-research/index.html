Executive summary

The highest-value next step is confidential due diligence, not a forecast based on a guessed address. A short remaining lease can affect buyer pricing, but the size and direction of the discount must be tested with comparable transactions and competing written offers. A renewal can preserve income but concentrates tenant, property and disaster risk. Neither is demonstrably superior on the available record.

Keep parcel identifiers, lease documents and owner finances with authorized advisers in an access-controlled offline workflow. This public site does not collect or store them. Do not enter them into third-party maps from a shared device.
