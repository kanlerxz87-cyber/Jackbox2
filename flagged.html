How to review efficiently

  1. First pass, 20 minutes. Read F-01, F-02, F-09 and F-10. They are the four that could change a number by more than a rounding error.
  2. Second pass, with counsel. F-12 to F-16 and F-19 require the parcel and the lease; do them in the secure workspace, not here.
  3. Third pass, with the accountant. F-06 to F-08 and the tax rows on Sell vs hold.
  4. Record the outcome. Each resolved row should be dated and the matching ledger row updated, so the site never shows a blank that has already been answered.

What is not flagged, and why

  • The archive pages. They are other sessions' work and are described, not audited, here.
  • Statutory rates that were verified. They are in the ledger with their sources, not in this queue.
