Conflicts between official sources, shown rather than resolved

F-18 LA County population in 2025: the Census estimate and the state estimate differ, because they use different methods for different reference dates. Census via FRED — 9,694,934 at 1 July 2025 CA Dept. of Finance — loss of 64,000 in the year to 1 January 2026 Both figures are published side by side on Trade Area with their reference dates and methods. Neither is averaged. The direction, population decline, is agreed by both.
F-19 LA area consumer prices for October 2026: the 12-month change is not published, because October 2025 data were not collected during the federal funding lapse. BLS — October 2026 release, 12-month cell shown as "-" BLS — September 2026 release, where the 12-month change is +3.7% The site quotes the September 2026 12-month change and leaves October's 12-month change blank rather than computing a substitute from a year-earlier figure that does not exist.
