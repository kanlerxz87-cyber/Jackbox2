01 · Decision brief

The decision, in plain language

For an anonymous Jack in the Box property described as having a triple-net lease expiring in the 2030–2035 window. The lease and exact location have not been verified.

No winner selected
Interim recommendation

Preserve optionality; verify before committing.

Do not choose a sale or renewal on regional indicators alone. Ask counsel to commission a private lease abstract and reconcile every notice right; simultaneously obtain comparable short-lease and renewal quotes, an accountant’s tax analysis, and a parcel-specific hazard and insurance review. This is a process recommendation, not a valuation or individualized financial advice.

Only after those inputs exist should the owner and advisers compare after-tax sale equity with the present value of after-tax hold cash flows, alongside liquidity, concentration, estate needs and acceptable downside.

How to make the eventual choice

Use the same date, after-tax equity basis and risk limits for each case.

A sale may be preferable if…

Executable after-tax proceeds and a realistic reinvestment plan meet the owner’s liquidity and diversification goals and compare favorably with a risk-adjusted hold case.

A renewal may be preferable if…

A signed, credit-supported renewal and realistic downside stresses justify keeping concentrated exposure and compare favorably with after-tax sale proceeds.

A larger modeled value is not automatically the best personal decision. Match the owner’s documented risk tolerance, cash needs and time horizon; no personal objectives are available here.
