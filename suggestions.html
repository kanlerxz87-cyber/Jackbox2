Suggestions

This page exists so that recommendations can be reviewed and chosen rather than silently applied. Each suggestion states what it would add, what it costs in time or money, and what it would change about the decision. Nothing below has been implemented. Pick the ones you want and they can be built; ignore the rest.

How to choose. Read the "decision value" column, not the "effort" column. A high-value, low-effort item is worth starting today. A low-value, high-effort item is worth ignoring even if it sounds thorough. Three suggestions below are marked as the ones that would actually change the answer.

B · Decision strategies worth weighing

These are the actual paths available. Each is stated with the conditions under which it is the right choice, so you can see which conditions you meet.

#StrategyChoose it whenPrincipal costPrincipal benefit
D-01 Renew early with a shorter term and a fixed option The lease abstract shows the tenant controls the extension, the store covers rent comfortably, and the renewal rent is at or below market. You keep the asset, avoid tax, and keep the option to sell later. You lock in a rent for several years with a tenant whose sales are still falling. Avoids the entire tax bill, preserves the land option and keeps the income.
D-02 Sell now, before the term shortens further The after-tax number beats the stressed hold case by a margin larger than your pricing error, and the parcel's hazard or insurance profile is weak. Large and irreversible tax leakage, including Measure ULA on gross price if inside the City. Removes single-tenant, single-site and seismic concentration in one transaction.
D-03 Sell into a §1031 exchange You want out of this asset but not out of real estate, and you have identified a credible replacement with better fundamentals. 45-day identification and 180-day closing deadlines; a replacement that may be worse than what you sold. Defers federal and California income tax and recapture, while diversifying away from this parcel.
D-04 Hold to expiry, then re-tenant or redevelop Zoning permits a materially higher-value use, the site is in a transit corridor, and you can carry twelve months of vacancy without a forced sale. Highest short-lease discount, re-tenanting cost, and complete loss of rent at expiry. Captures land value that a burger tenant's rent cannot express.
D-05 Do nothing until the gates clear Always, for the next two to three weeks. This is the recommendation today. A few thousand dollars and some time. Converts a guess into a decision, and prevents the two expensive mistakes: selling too cheap and renewing into a rent the store cannot cover.

C · Things this site deliberately does not do

Not done, on purpose

  • No automated valuation or algorithm-derived price estimate. Every AVM in commercial property is an interpolation over stale comparables.
  • No fill-in of missing values from a data vendor. Vendors are not official sources and their errors are indistinguishable from their data.
  • No recommendation of one strategy as "the" answer while the private inputs are unknown.
  • No collection of any data from you. The calculator is client-side only.
  • No personal or parcel-identifying information anywhere in the repository.

Available if you want it

  • A tract-level build once the APN is supplied privately (S-01).
  • A hazard overlay once the address is available (S-02).
  • A broker-quote tracker once quotes exist (S-03).
  • A notice calendar once the abstract exists (S-04).
  • A versioned cash-flow model once verified inputs exist (S-05).
  • A replacement-property screener if an exchange becomes live (S-06).
Recommended next three actions, in order. (1) Send the abstract request to an attorney today — it gates everything and takes the longest to come back. (2) Ask a CPA for the basis and depreciation schedule in the same week. (3) Do the free hazard lookups yourself this week, because they cost nothing and remove the most uncertainty per hour spent. Everything else on this page can wait until those three are done.
