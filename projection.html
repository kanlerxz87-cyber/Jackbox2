Research design · Version 0.1

A projection system, not a prediction

No reliable parcel forecast or back-test is possible with the available evidence. This specification sets out what must exist before a model is built and used.

Minimum model-development protocol

Source links support the data or risk topic; the methodology itself is a proposed analytical design.
