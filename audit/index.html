Audit scope P-01 · 2026-11-26

Public-site integrity audit

This page distinguishes a local publication-and-provenance check from verification of a property fact. A working link or an allowed official domain is not proof that a private lease, parcel, owner financial record, quote, or tax calculation is correct. Those items stay blank and flagged.

Current dashboard: consolidated root workspace No outcome selected Earlier dashboards are dated archive copies

What this audit checks

Privacy signals

Scans public HTML, JSON, CSS, JavaScript, and Markdown for common SSN, email, phone, ZIP, street-address, and parcel-number patterns. It is a safety net, not a complete DLP solution.

Link integrity

Checks local targets and anchors, blocks insecure HTTP and browser-facing localhost links, and reports direct official-source-link coverage for evidence tables.

Evidence versus registers

The consolidated root checker requires every evidence row to carry a source. Tables explicitly labelled as registers, methods, legends, or archives are reported separately rather than misrepresented as evidence.

Honest boundary

No public automation can validate private lease terms, parcel-zone status, broker quotes, tax basis, or owner objectives. Those are decision gates, not assumptions.

Decision rule remains unchanged

Do not select sell, renew, or hold from public context alone. A defensible choice requires verified private inputs and aligned dates: lease abstract and notice calendar, tenant or guarantor obligation, rent and capex obligations, parcel-level hazards and insurance, broker bids and renewal terms, owner tax basis, depreciation and entity facts, debt or reinvestment constraints, and written owner objectives.
