How to use it honestly

  1. Enter only verified inputs (lease abstract, CPA basis, broker quotes). Leave unknowns blank — the tool will show $0, which is a signal to stop, not a valuation.
  2. Run the short-lease discount sensitivity at several levels (e.g. 5% / 10% / 15% / 20%) until real quotes replace the assumption.
  3. Stress the hold case: 12-month rent loss, major repair, and a 200 bps cap-rate widening at exit — together, not separately.
