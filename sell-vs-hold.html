W-02 · Optional adviser worksheet

Sell vs. hold, after tax

No input is needed to browse this site. This optional worksheet accepts adviser-prepared numbers only; it does not calculate taxes, appraise the property, validate inputs, or select a winner.

Blank until verified inputs exist
Private by design, not a secure vault. The worksheet code has no network or storage functions and has no submission form. Inputs stay in the current page’s memory, but the hosting provider, browser, extensions, device and external sites have their own privacy risks. Use a trusted device and, where practical, a trusted offline copy. Do not enter names, addresses, lease text or identifiers. Clear the page when finished.

Sale, today

Gross sale price − transaction/transfer costs − debt payoff − CPA-calculated incremental income tax = after-tax sale equity today. Avoid double-counting taxes or costs.

Hold, same valuation date

Present value of each year’s after-tax cash flow plus terminal after-tax sale equity. Annual flows and terminal equity must already include the relevant tax, costs and debt, with no double count.
