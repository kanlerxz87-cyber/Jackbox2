Parcel hazard screening

Regional context is not a substitute for the parcel's actual zone status. Each hazard below has an official lookup tool; the parcel result is blank until the address/APN is checked offline with authorised advisers.

What to check privately (engineering + insurance)

  • Fault-rupture / liquefaction / landslide zones for the parcel (CGS maps via authorised adviser).
  • FEMA flood zone letter + whether flood insurance is required / available.
  • CAL FIRE FHSZ designation + defensible-space / brush-clearance obligations.
  • Insurance quotes: property, earthquake (CEA), flood, business interruption — stress loss of rent + repairs + exit liquidity together (see Risks).
  • Seismic retrofit / deferred-maintenance report (licensed engineer, private).
Do not price the asset as if hazards were zero. Until parcel zones are confirmed, any valuation must carry a hazard contingency and the Flagged page must keep these rows blank.
