W-04 · Physical risk

Screen the parcel—not the region

Map designations, structural vulnerability and insurance coverage are different questions. All subject-specific results remain private and unverified.

Hazard screening register

The linked sources explain the screening tools; they do not establish this parcel’s zone or insured loss.

ExposureParcel statusPrivate next stepOfficial source / reference

Map limitations: CGS says its maps do not show every hazard and do not replace site-specific investigations. CAL FIRE distinguishes hazard from risk. A location outside a mapped zone is not proof of zero loss.

Stress the combined loss

An event may affect income and the ability to sell at the same time.

01 · Income

Loss of rent

Review abatement, interruption coverage, tenant obligations, repair time and uninsured downtime together.

02 · Cost

Repairs & deductibles

Include structural repairs, deductibles, uncovered expenses and financing needs in the same scenario.

03 · Exit

Liquidity shock

Consider delayed sale, weaker pricing and insurance availability after the same event. Do not assume independence.
