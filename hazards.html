The three stresses, run together

The brief asks for loss of rent, repairs and exit liquidity to be stressed together rather than one at a time. That is the right instruction, because they arrive together.

StressDriverHow to size itWhat it does to the decision
Loss of rentBuilding unusable, tenant not trading, or tenant refuses to pay during repair Months of downtime from the engineer, multiplied by rent plus recoverable costs, less the loss-of-rent limit and the waiting period If the cover does not reach the downtime, the owner funds the gap and the hold case weakens materially.
RepairsStructural, façade, utilities, site works Engineer's order-of-magnitude range plus a contingency, less the deductible A repair bill near the property's net value makes the hold case a bet on insurance, not on real estate.
Exit liquidityBuyers withdraw or demand a discount after an event Assume no sale for at least two quarters after a declared disaster, and a wider cap rate on the eventual sale If the owner needs liquidity during that window, the sale happens at the worst price. That is the real risk.

Insurance questions that decide more than the premium

  • Is loss of rent covered, for how many months, and does the waiting period start at the event or at the end of the repair?
  • Are earthquake, flood and wildfire covered at all, and are any of them excluded by endorsement?
  • Is the deductible a fixed amount or a percentage of insured value? A percentage deductible scales with the problem.
  • Does the policy require a seismic retrofit or a roof condition as a warranty? A warranty breach can void a claim after the loss.
  • What happens at renewal if the carrier withdraws from the area? A FAIR Plan comparison answers the availability question, not the adequacy question.

Governance rule proposed for a declared event

Proposed, not adopted. Write down now that no sale will be signed within 90 days of a declared disaster affecting the parcel, and that the decision will be re-underwritten from the hazard file rather than from the pre-event valuation. The value of the rule is that it is decided while calm.

What would change this page from framework to finding

  1. Four dated determinations from the official sources above.
  2. An engineering opinion with an order-of-magnitude repair range and a downtime estimate.
  3. Two insurance quotes plus a FAIR Plan comparison, with deductibles and exclusions.
  4. A written statement of the owner's liquidity outside this property, because exit liquidity is a function of the owner's balance sheet, not only the market's.
