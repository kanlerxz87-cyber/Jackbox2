Parcel hazards: what is known, and what only the parcel can tell you

Regional hazard context is published and verifiable. The parcel's actual zone status is not, and cannot be until someone looks it up for that specific address. This page separates the two and refuses to blur them.

The distinction that matters. "Los Angeles has high seismic risk" is true, verified, and useless for underwriting. "This parcel is or is not in a fault-rupture zone, a liquefaction zone, a FEMA special flood hazard area, or a state fire hazard severity zone" changes the insurance cost, the capex reserve, the exit price and possibly the financeability. Only the second kind of fact should move your decision.

3 · Why the 2025 wildfire season matters even for an urban parcel

The January 2025 Palisades and Eaton fires were not in your trade area, but they changed the California insurance market for every commercial property in the state, including urban ones.

Two consequences for this decision. First, an insurance quote obtained today may not be renewable on the same terms at the next renewal, so the hold case should carry an insurance inflation assumption, not a flat premium. Second, if the parcel's hazard profile turns out to be elevated, the buyer pool narrows at exactly the moment you want to sell — which argues for testing the market while the lease still has meaningful term.

What to do with this page. Take the ten blank rows above to your insurance broker and your engineer as a checklist. Each answer either confirms that hazard is a normal cost of ownership or converts it into a line item that must be deducted from the hold case on Sell vs Hold. Do not price the asset as though the hazard exposure were zero, and do not assume it is severe either — the point is that nobody currently knows, and one afternoon of lookups settles it.
