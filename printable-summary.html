The question

Sell before expiry, renew, hold to expiry for re-tenanting, or sell into a §1031 exchange — and which of those best preserves capital while keeping room for growth at a manageable level of risk.

The answer today

Neither sell nor renew is defensible on current evidence. Public sources establish the tenant's direction and the local economy. They do not establish the lease terms, the owner's tax basis, the parcel's hazard zone or the market price of the remaining term. Each of those can independently reverse the conclusion.

What each adviser needs to supply

AdviserDeliverableWhy it is the gating item
Real estate attorney Written lease abstract: expiry, options, notice windows, rent schedule, escalation, guaranty, assignment restrictions, capital obligations, casualty and condemnation terms. Sets the timeline and reveals whether the tenant or the owner controls the extension.
CPA Adjusted basis, accumulated depreciation, holding entity, effective California rate, and after-tax proceeds at two or three candidate prices including one below a Measure ULA threshold. Without it the sale case cannot be computed at all.
Insurance broker Property, earthquake, flood and business-interruption quotes, with deductibles stated in dollars, plus a renewal indication. Premium and deductible are direct deductions from the hold case.
Structural engineer Retrofit status and a repair estimate for roof, structure and accessibility. Converts an unknown into a reserve figure the stress test can use.
Broker Two or three written opinions stating both a long-lease and a short-lease value, plus a market-rent indication for renewal. The short-lease discount cannot be derived from public data.
