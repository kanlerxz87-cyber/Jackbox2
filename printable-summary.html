Recommended next three actions

  1. Abstract the lease and build the dated notice calendar (gate G1).
  2. Request an estoppel certificate and a written renewal indication, plus store-level financials (gate G2).
  3. Order three broker opinions with short-lease comparables and a re-leasing study (gate G5).

What must not be done

  • Do not sign a renewal, or list the property, before the lease abstract and the notice calendar exist.
  • Do not quote a cap rate, discount or sale price that is not backed by a comparable or a written opinion.
  • Do not put the parcel identifier, lease file or owner finances into any public document, including this one.
  • Do not treat a county statistic as a trade-area fact.
