Print summary

Non-negotiable privacy boundary

This public repository is not a private workspace. Keep all parcel/owner identifiers, executed lease documents, exact rent, tax basis, owner finances, and contact details offline in an access-controlled workflow with authorized advisers. Do not enter sensitive information on this website. Publish only reviewed, aggregate, non-identifying findings.

Suggested sequence

  1. Have counsel prepare a private lease abstract and reconcile every option and notice date to the calendar.
  2. Ask a CPA to verify basis, depreciation, entity/filing assumptions, and after-tax sale and hold cash flows.
  3. Obtain multiple written broker indications for current lease term and negotiated renewal cases; record quotes and assumptions privately.
  4. In the secure workflow, select consistent Census tracts and verify planning, traffic, school, competitor, and parcel hazard evidence.
  5. Only after those gates, compare scenarios and document the owner's risk limits, liquidity needs, and diversification goals with advisers.

This workspace is an evidence organizer, not legal, tax, investment, insurance, appraisal, or financial advice. Unknowns are deliberately not filled by inference.
