What is verified, missing and unverified

Verified

Tenant financials, county and metro economics, federal and California tax rules, LA transfer tax and the regional hazard record. Each is linked to the issuing agency above.

Missing (private)

Lease terms and notice dates, your basis and depreciation, the parcel's actual hazard zone, insurance quotes, and real broker pricing for a short-lease QSR. These decide the answer and cannot be published.

Unverified

Trade-area (census-tract) figures, school and traffic detail, competitor counts, and the short-lease discount. Blank on this site by design, with the lookup route listed on Flagged for Review.

Privacy: what this site will never show

No parcel address, no APN, no owner name or entity, no lease document, no rent roll, no bank or loan details and no tax returns appear anywhere in this repository or on this site. The calculator is a static page: it performs no network requests, sets no cookies and stores nothing. Anything you type is discarded when you close the tab. Keep the identifiers and documents in the access-controlled workspace with your advisers.
