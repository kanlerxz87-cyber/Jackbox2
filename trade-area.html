How to interpret tract trends

Keep observations, uncertainty and assumptions separate.

Consistent boundaries

Use non-overlapping ACS five-year periods, account for boundary changes and report margins of error. A period estimate is not a single-year reading.

No false precision

Do not treat a county average, arbitrary radius, school score or traffic count as this location’s sales or value. Review the catchment and source limitations with a qualified analyst.
