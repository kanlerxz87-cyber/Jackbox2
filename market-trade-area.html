4-Step Offline Protocol to Replace Regional Proxies With Trade-Area Evidence

Follow privately on an access-controlled workstation; publish only aggregate, non-identifying summaries if ever shared.

Step 1 · Jurisdiction & Zoning Check (Offline)

  • Confirm whether confidential APN sits inside incorporated City of Los Angeles (triggers Measure ULA >$5.4M & LA City $18.42/hr wage) or adjacent city/unincorporated LA County.
  • Check LA City ZIMAS for Zoning Code, Community Plan Area, Measure JJJ TOC Tier (1–4), AB 2011 commercial corridor eligibility, and grandfathered drive-thru status.

Step 2 · Tract-Level Trend Evidence (Offline)

  • Geocode confidential address privately via U.S. Census Geocoder to identify the home Census tract and contiguous 1-mile / 3-mile tracts.
  • Pull 2015–2019 vs. 2020–2024 ACS 5-Year tables (B01003 Population, B19013 Median HH Income, B25003 Tenure, B25064 Gross Rent, B17001 Poverty) to verify whether the immediate neighborhood is gentrifying/densifying or contracting relative to LA City.

Step 3 · Traffic, Transit & School Catchment

  • Verify arterial AADT via Caltrans Traffic Census / LADOT NavigateLA, corner signalization, curb cuts, and drive-thru lane stacking depth.
  • Measure distance to nearest LA Metro rail station (D/B/E/A/K Lines) and check assigned local LAUSD schools on CA Dept. of Education Dashboard.

Step 4 · QSR Competitor & Cannibalization Audit

  • Map all drive-thru QSRs within 1.5 miles (McDonald's, In-N-Out, Taco Bell, Chick-fil-A, Raising Cane's, Burger King, Carl's Jr.) AND other nearby Jack in the Box units (cannibalization risk under "JACK on Track").
  • Pair with offline store sales / broker foot-traffic data before Gate-4 renewal or sale pricing.
