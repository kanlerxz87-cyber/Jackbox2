Why "delivered as a method" is the honest label

Four requirements — trade-area figures, the sell-versus-hold comparison, parcel hazard status and the neighbourhood data — cannot be finished without records that identify the property. Publishing those records would breach the owner's first instruction, which outranks the analytical instruction. The compromise is deliberate: publish the rule, the formula, the official lookup and the blank, so a reader can see exactly what remains and who holds it. That is more useful than a number with no provenance, and it is the only version of this project that respects both instructions at once.
