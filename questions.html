Questions

Every question that must be answered before a decision, who holds the answer, which document contains it, and what changes depending on what it says.

The short answer to "can a decision be made?" is no, and these are the reasons. 28 questions stand between the evidence in hand and a decision that can be defended. They are grouped below by area, each with the person who can answer it, the document that answers it, the gate it belongs to, and what changes depending on the answer. The first four are urgent because delay itself forecloses options.

The four that cannot wait

  1. Q-01 and Q-02 — exact expiry, options and notice dates. Everything else is analysis; this is deadline management. A notice period that passes removes a choice permanently.
  2. Q-07 — has the tenant said anything about renewal? The renewal branch is currently an assumption, not a position.
  3. Q-23 — adjusted basis and depreciation. Without them, the sale number is pre-tax and therefore wrong by an unknown amount.
  4. Q-16 to Q-19 — the hazard and insurance file. An uninsurable loss is the only event that can force a sale at the worst moment.
