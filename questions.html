Questions before any decision

Every answer below is blank because the source evidence is not available to this public site. A linked official reference supplies context, not the private answer.

Open questions and discovery routes

All answers are unverified; the role/action describes a suggested way to obtain them privately.

QuestionAnswerSuggested discovery actionOfficial source / reference
Q01 · When does the base lease end?—UnverifiedAttorney obtains the executed lease and every amendment; records the exact date privately.SEC · FY2025 10-K ↗
Q02 · Who controls renewal options?—UnverifiedAttorney identifies each option, conditions, rent-setting method and whether the right remains exercisable.SEC · FY2025 10-K ↗
Q03 · What are the notice deadlines and delivery rules?—UnverifiedCounsel calculates dates from the actual contract, confirms recipient and service method, and reconciles an independent private calendar.SEC · FY2025 10-K ↗
Q04 · Who is the tenant and who guarantees rent?—UnverifiedCounsel checks the contracting entity, guaranty, assignments and estoppel; do not infer from the brand.SEC · FY2025 10-K ↗
Q05 · What rent and escalation actually apply?—UnverifiedReconcile the rent roll, amendments, payment history and any percentage-rent clause privately.SEC · FY2025 10-K ↗
Q06 · What does NNN really allocate?—UnverifiedAbstract taxes, insurance, common expenses, roof, structure, HVAC, environmental and capital obligations.SEC · FY2025 10-K ↗
Q07 · What happens after casualty or interruption?—UnverifiedReview restoration, rent abatement, termination, insurance proceeds and repair responsibility in the lease.CA Geological Survey · EQ Zapp ↗ · FEMA · flood maps ↗
Q08 · What restrictions affect sale or financing?—UnverifiedCounsel reviews assignment, consent, first-offer rights, easements, use restrictions, title and existing loan terms.SEC · FY2025 10-K ↗
Q09 · How healthy is the actual restaurant?—UnverifiedRequest permitted unit-level sales, rent coverage and guarantor financial information privately; compare with system disclosures.SEC · Q3 FY2026 release ↗
Q10 · What renewal could actually be negotiated?—UnverifiedObtain a non-binding written proposal including rent, term, guarantee, options, concessions and capital obligations.SEC · FY2025 10-K ↗
Q11 · What are executable short- and long-lease prices?—UnverifiedObtain multiple contemporaneous, written, matched broker opinions; document terms, comps and uncertainty.SEC · FY2025 10-K ↗
Q12 · What is the owner’s adjusted basis and tax?—UnverifiedCPA reconciles acquisition records, improvements, depreciation, entity, losses, income and federal/state consequences privately.IRS · capital gains ↗ · CA FTB · real-estate withholding ↗
Q13 · What debt and exit costs are due?—UnverifiedReview payoff, prepayment, brokerage, transfer tax, escrow and the relevant sale-date jurisdiction.LA Finance · Measure ULA ↗ · CA FTB · real-estate withholding ↗
Q14 · Is an exchange viable?—UnverifiedCPA and qualified intermediary assess eligibility, replacement supply, financing and identification/receipt deadlines before any closing.IRS · Form 8824 instructions ↗ · CA FTB · real-estate withholding ↗
Q15 · What liquidity and risk tolerance are appropriate?—UnverifiedAdviser documents cash needs, concentration, investment horizon, estate aims and acceptable loss privately.SEC · diversification ↗
Q16 · What is the relevant catchment?—UnverifiedA trusted analyst uses the withheld location to set a consistent trade area and tracts without publishing the location.Census · ACS 5-year data ↗ · Census · ACS comparison guidance ↗
Q17 · How have local income and population changed?—UnverifiedRetrieve matching non-overlapping tract estimates and margins of error; document boundary and price-level changes.Census · ACS comparison guidance ↗
Q18 · What is the local employment and traffic picture?—UnverifiedCombine appropriately bounded employment evidence with city street counts or relevant state highway counts.BLS · LA metro data ↗ · Caltrans · traffic census ↗
Q19 · What schools and competitors affect the catchment?—UnverifiedUse CDE and a dated private competitor survey; avoid treating school metrics as proof of restaurant demand.CA Education · DataQuest ↗ · LA Planning · case reports ↗
Q20 · What land use, transit or development changes apply?—UnverifiedConfirm jurisdiction, zoning, access and official plans; distinguish proposals from approved or completed work.LA Planning · case reports ↗
Q21 · What is the parcel’s earthquake exposure?—UnverifiedCheck CGS zones and commissioning of a building-specific engineering and ground assessment.CA Geological Survey · EQ Zapp ↗
Q22 · What are the flood and fire designations?—UnverifiedReview the effective FEMA and adopted local/state fire maps privately; record map date and limitations.FEMA · flood maps ↗ · CAL FIRE · hazard zones ↗
Q23 · What insurance and reserves exist?—UnverifiedObtain policy limits, exclusions, deductibles, loss-of-rent terms and actual quotes; compare with repair and downtime estimates.FEMA · flood maps ↗ · CAL FIRE · hazard zones ↗
Q24 · What happens in a compound disaster?—UnverifiedStress lost rent, uncovered repairs and weaker exit liquidity together; compare with private reserve capacity.USGS · regional earthquake model ↗ · FEMA · flood maps ↗ · CAL FIRE · hazard zones ↗
Q25 · Is a projection actually reliable?—UnverifiedCollect sufficient compatible history, freeze versions and run a genuine walk-forward back-test before using projections.Census · ACS comparison guidance ↗ · Census · county population ↗
