Jack in the Box · Central Los Angeles Questions, answers and the plan to close each gap

What still has to be answered before you can decide

Twenty-two questions. For each one: whether it is answered today, what the answer would change, and exactly how to get it. Nothing here asks you to type anything into this site. These are questions for your attorney, your CPA, your broker and your insurer, and the answers stay in your private workspace.

Read the "what it changes" column first. If an answer would not change the decision, it is not worth paying for. Every question below is on this list because the answer could flip the sell-versus-renew conclusion, change the price by a material amount, or create a legal exposure.

Open: the lease

#QuestionWhat it changesHow to answer itStatus
Q1What is the exact expiry date, and does the tenant hold options to extend? The whole timeline. An option the tenant controls changes who has leverage; a fixed expiry gives you the choice.Lease abstract from a real estate attorney.Open
Q2What are the notice windows for renewal, termination and rent review, and what happens if one is missed? A missed notice can forfeit an option entirely, or auto-extend a lease you wanted to end. This is the single largest avoidable risk in the whole file.Same abstract, with every date diarised. Open
Q3What is the current rent, the escalation formula, and any percentage-rent clause with its threshold? Drives both the hold value and the price a buyer will pay. Percentage rent has been falling system-wide, so a stale threshold is worth renegotiating.Lease plus rent roll.Open
Q4Is there a guaranty, and who is behind it — the corporate entity or a single-asset franchisee? Determines whether the rent claim survives a tenant failure. A franchisee guaranty is worth far less than a corporate one.Lease, guaranty and tenant entity documents; a credit check on the guarantor. Open
Q5Who pays for roof, structure, parking lot and code upgrades, and are there remodel obligations on renewal? In an NNN lease the answer is not automatic. A hidden capital obligation can wipe out a year of rent. Lease maintenance and alteration clauses, read by counsel.Open
Q6Does the lease restrict assignment, change of use, or sale of the property? Affects how easily you can sell and what a buyer can do with the site afterwards. Lease assignment and transfer clauses.Open
Q7What are the holdover, abatement and condemnation provisions? Governs what happens if the tenant stays past expiry, if a casualty stops business, or if a public project takes part of the site.Lease casualty and condemnation clauses.Open

#QuestionWhat it changesHow to answer itStatus
Q8What is the adjusted basis and how much depreciation has been taken? The entire sale calculation. Recapture at 25% on every dollar of depreciation can dominate the tax bill. CPA-prepared schedule from the depreciation records.Open
Q9What entity holds the property, and what is the effective California rate on a sale? An entity sale, an instalment sale or a §1031 exchange produce very different after-tax numbers. CPA and attorney, based on the holding entity.Open
Q10Would the sale price cross the $5.4M or $10.9M Measure ULA threshold? A price just above a threshold triggers a step change in tax; a price just below avoids it. This is a legitimate structuring consideration, and a large one. Broker price range plus the LA Office of Finance rate table.Open
Q11Is there debt on the property, and would it be repaid or assumed? Affects net proceeds, and a prepayment penalty can be material.Loan statement and payoff letter. Open
Q12Is there a §1031 replacement identified, and is the 45-day window feasible? The exchange is only worth doing if a replacement exists. Without one, the deadlines create risk rather than save tax.Attorney and qualified intermediary, before listing. Open
Q13Do you need liquidity from this asset within a defined period? If yes, the decision is largely made and the question becomes timing, not whether. Your own answer, with your adviser.Owner input

Q14What is the APN, and which Census tracts and block groups contain the parcel? Unlocks every trade-area figure and therefore the local demand case. County recorder plus the Census geocoder, in a private session.Open

Q18What would property, earthquake, flood and business-interruption cover actually cost, on what deductible? A direct deduction from the hold case, and a diligence item for any buyer. Two independent broker quotes, with deductibles in dollars.Open

How to close these quickly

One attorney call

Closes Q1 to Q7. Ask for a written abstract, not an oral summary, and ask specifically about every notice date and whether missing one forfeits anything.

One CPA call

Closes Q8, Q9, Q11 and most of Q12. Ask for the after-tax proceeds at two or three price points, including one just below a Measure ULA threshold.

One afternoon of lookups

Closes Q14 to Q17 and Q19 using the official tools linked on Hazards and Trade Area. Free, and it removes most of the uncertainty about hazard.

Two broker conversations

Closes Q10, Q21 and Q22, and gives you Q20 in practice. Ask for written opinions, dated, with the short-lease discount stated explicitly.

What "answered" means. An answer counts when a named professional has put it in writing and you have filed it. A verbal indication from a broker is not an answer; neither is a Zillow estimate, a county assessor's roll value, or a figure from a data vendor. Those inputs are how people end up selling a good asset at a bad price or renewing into a rent the store cannot cover.
