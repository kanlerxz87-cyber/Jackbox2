Decision gates

Seven gates in order, each with the evidence required to clear it and the evidence in hand. Nothing is committed while a gate is open.

Rule of the board. A gate clears only on evidence, never on opinion, and no gate may be skipped. Value work (G5) and tax work (G6) may run in parallel with the lease work, but the comparison in G7 cannot be signed until every earlier gate is closed. Three gates are blocked for one reason: the private inputs are not in this repository and must not be.

Why the gates are in this order

The lease comes first because it is the only gate where delay destroys value by default: a notice date can pass while the analysis is still running. The tenant and the valuation come next because they set the range. Hazard and insurance come before the tax work only because an uninsurable asset changes the question being asked, not just the number. The tax work is last among the inputs because it needs the sale price, and the sale price needs the valuation.
