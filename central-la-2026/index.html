Executive Summary: What Official Evidence Shows Today (Nov 25, 2026)

Synthesizing verified public facts across tenant credit, LA macroeconomics, taxes, and catastrophic tail risk.

Open W-10 1-Page Printable Brief

1. Tenant Trajectory & Refinancing Risk (SEC Verified)

  • Multi-year sales & unit contraction: System same-store sales fell from +7.3% (FY23) to -1.3% (FY24), -4.2% (FY25), and stayed negative in FY26 (Q1 -6.7%, Q2 -3.8%, Q3 -1.1%). Total units shrank from 2,191 (FY24) to 2,115 (Q3 FY26) under the "JACK on Track" closure plan.
  • Franchise rental revenue declining: Standalone Jack in the Box franchise rental revenue dropped from $351.3M (FY23) to $332.7M (FY25), and 40-wk YTD FY26 fell -6.8% YoY ($242.5M vs. $260.3M).
  • Higher debt cost colliding with 2030–2035 lease window: After selling Del Taco (Dec 22, 2025) for $119.1M net proceeds, JACK issued $500M of Series 2026-1 notes at 7.624% (vs. 3.445%–4.476% repaid debt) with an Anticipated Repayment Date of May 2031, while Senior ABS leverage >5.25x triggered $23.3M mandatory cash sweeps.
  • Leadership transition: An interim CEO appointment was disclosed in May 2026; CFO, Chief Legal Officer, and CTO departures were announced in November 2026 (Form 8-K).

2. Central LA Economic & Regulatory Reality

  • Population & jobs: CA DOF estimates LA City fell -0.9% YoY to 3,806,201 and LA County lost 64,000 residents (-0.6%) to 9,837,286 by Jan 2026; BLS Sep 2026 MSA payroll growth was +0.5% YoY (statistically flat).
  • Operating cost pressure: CA AB 1228 imposes a $20.00/hr fast-food wage (44.1% of JACK stores are in California: 941 units), while the City of LA general minimum wage is $18.42/hr from Jul 2026 where that ordinance applies (official city notice). Do not add minimum-wage rates together; confirm the applicable rule for the worksite.
  • Structural land-value upside: Metro D Line Section 1 (Wilshire/La Brea, Fairfax, La Cienega) opened May 8, 2026, with Sections 2–3 forecast 2027 ahead of the 2028 Olympics — reinforcing Central LA transit-corridor zoning value (TOC Tiers 1–4 & CA AB 2011).

3. Tax Friction & Wealth Preservation Math

  • Taxable-sale taxes require individualized modeling: Federal long-term capital-gain rates and the maximum rate on unrecaptured §1250 gain apply to different gain components; NIIT and California income tax depend on the owner's circumstances. Local transfer taxes are separate; if the property is within the City of Los Angeles, its official Measure ULA thresholds and rates may also apply. Do not add statutory maximum rates as if they were one flat tax rate.
  • §1031 vs. Blend-and-Extend: An IRC §1031 exchange defers federal & CA income/recapture tax (not transfer/ULA tax) while diversifying away from single-site LA risk; a negotiated blend-and-extend avoids immediate tax & ULA but preserves single-tenant & LA seismic exposure.
  • Prop 19 estate constraint: California Prop 19 eliminated parent-child property tax reassessment exclusions for commercial rental properties — critical for multi-generational hold planning.

4. Catastrophic Black Swan & Insurance Stress

  • Seismic tail risk: USGS UCERF3 places 30-year Los Angeles earthquake probabilities at 60% (≥M6.7), 46% (≥M7.0), and 31% (≥M7.5).
  • Wildfire & insurance shock: Jan 2025 Palisades (6,845 structures destroyed) and Eaton (9,414 destroyed) fires prompted CDI Order 2025-1 ($1B FAIR Plan assessment) and Order 2026-1 ($600M credit line). Commercial property insurance availability & deductibles must be audited privately.
  • Immediate next steps: Complete Gates 0–4 (Attorney Lease Abstract, Offline W-01/W-04 Parcel Checks, Store Sales Review, 3 Broker BOVs + CPA Tax Run) before selecting a winning path.
