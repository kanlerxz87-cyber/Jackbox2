Quote the lease discount

A practical protocol, not an invented percentage.

Ask qualified brokers or buyers for dated, written indications on the same asset: (1) sale with the current remaining lease and (2) sale conditional on a specified signed renewal. Align sale date, rent, expenses, credit, contingencies and transaction costs. Difference in net quotes is an observed indication, not a causal estimate; reconcile with independent comparables. Keep all identifying materials offline.
