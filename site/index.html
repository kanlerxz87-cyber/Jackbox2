The short version

What we can say with verified evidence

  • Regional indicators are context, not this parcel's trade area. Metro jobs and city population use different geographies and cannot establish site demand or a reliable local projection.
  • Tenant-system results are not the subject store's results. Public filings do not establish the lease obligor, guaranty, store sales, rent coverage or renewal intent for this parcel.
  • Market rates do not price this lease. A Treasury yield is not a property cap rate or short-lease discount; real offers and comparable evidence are missing.
  • Tax provisions do not select a path. Any exchange or disposition treatment depends on the actual owner, basis, transaction and eligibility, to be modelled by a CPA.

What we cannot say, and will not guess

  • The rent. Unknown.
  • The term. Given as a five-year range, which cannot be discounted.
  • The obligor. Brand or franchisee — different balance sheets entirely.
  • The basis. Unknown, so the tax layer is unknown.
  • The price. No quote, no comparable, no verified spread.
  • The hazards. No parcel-level zone status for seismic, flood or fire.

No winner can be selected yet. The missing parcel, lease, offer, tax and owner-objective inputs are decision-critical. Neither aggregate economic indicators nor tenant-wide disclosures are enough to choose sell, renew or defer. Start with the decision process.

How to read this site

Each row of evidence carries one of four labels, and the label is the point:

  • observed — a number printed by an official source, quoted as-is.
  • derived — arithmetic on observations, with the formula shown so you can check it.
  • verified rule — a statement of law or regulation from a primary source.
  • template — a formula awaiting inputs. The value column is empty.
  • unverified — no official value was obtained. The value is left blank and the row is flagged. It is never estimated.

Every populated row links to the official source that printed the number. If a link is missing, the row is not populated. See the source ledger for the complete audit trail.

Scope and limits

What this is. A source-linked decision-support workspace for one property. It organises what is known, specifies exactly what is missing, and builds the comparison so that it can be completed the moment real inputs arrive.

What this is not. It is not an appraisal, a valuation, a broker's opinion, legal advice, or tax advice. It contains no price, because no verified price input exists. Any figure that would require an assumption is either absent or explicitly labelled as an assumption with the evidence that should replace it.

Research limitation, disclosed. This session could read web pages but could not download binary files or call data APIs. Where that blocked an official source — the California Department of Finance projection workbooks, the Census ACS API — the gap is recorded on the flagged page rather than filled with an estimate.

Privacy: this site publishes aggregate, non-identifying findings only. No owner name, entity, address, APN, lease document or financial record appears here, by design. All identifiers are replaced with placeholders. See the privacy policy.
