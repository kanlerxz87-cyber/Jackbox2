Jack in the Box · Central Los Angeles · single-tenant NNN ground lease

Adviser brief · prepared 25 November 2026 · all identifiers withheld · aggregate findings only

Bottom line

Do not commit to a sale, a renewal or a letter of intent yet. 0 of 12 decision gates are closed. The direction of the evidence is legible; the magnitude is not.

Lean if forced today: On the verified evidence, the weight of argument favours selling — but only after gates 1 and 2, and only with the 1031 path prepared in advance. If the objective statement prioritises income over liquidity and the lease has 8+ years of firm term with a strong obligor and a market-reset option, hold becomes defensible. Those two facts are unknown today, which is precisely why the answer is 'not yet'.

Verified evidence — the four points that matter

  • Area is not growing: metro employment +1.29% vs Oct 2019, +0.19% vs Oct 2024; city population −0.8% since 2020.
  • Tenant is shrinking: 86 closures FY2025, 50–100 guided FY2026, same-store sales −4.2% FY2025 and −7.4% Q4, ~7.3× leverage.
  • Capital is not cheap: 10-year Treasury 4.92% (23 Nov 2026).
  • Tax lever: §1031 deferral exists only if the owner sells; recapture at up to 25% plus 3.8% NIIT plus California up to 12.3%.

Recommended sequence

StepActionWhy this order
1Close every blocking gate (G-01, G-02, G-03, G-04, G-07, G-12). Nothing else happens first.Six unknowns entirely govern the answer.
2Obtain written quotes from three buyers AND a written renewal indication from the tenant, in the same 30-day window.This is the only way to compare a real sale price against a real renewal case rather than two templates.
3Ask the tenant, through the broker, whether it will buy the parcel.D-5 is the path most likely to produce the highest price and the fewest surprises, and it is almost never tested.
4Run the after-tax number with and without a section 1031 exchange, and identify replacement candidates before listing.The tax difference is large enough to change the decision, and the exchange must not be improvised.
5Decide against the owner's written objective function (G-12).Sell and hold optimise different things. That trade-off belongs to the owner and cannot be delegated to an analysis.

What would flip the recommendation to hold

  • A firm term extending meaningfully past 2032, with no tenant termination right.
  • A corporate or well-capitalised guaranty behind the rent.
  • A renewal option with a fair-market-value reset rather than a fixed bump.
  • Parcel-level hazard findings that are benign, plus affordable insurance.
  • Zoning headroom that gives the land a credible alternative use.
  • Three written quotes that come in materially above the owner's hold-case present value.

What is missing, in one list

  • Rent, escalation, exact expiry date, option mechanism
  • Obligor identity and guaranty
  • Owner basis and depreciation history
  • Parcel hazard status: seismic, flood, fire
  • Three written sale quotes; tenant's written renewal indication
  • Zoning and alternative-use headroom; trade-area figures
