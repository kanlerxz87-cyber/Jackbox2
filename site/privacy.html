Privacy policy

How personal and commercially sensitive information is kept out of this site structurally, not by convention.

The rule that governs every other decision on this site. Personal and commercially sensitive information is removed before publication, not after. Nothing is published and then redacted. The public site is assembled from a data layer that has never contained an identifier.

How privacy is enforced, not promised

  1. Structural separation. The site is generated from data/*.json. Those files contain no identifier fields at all — not empty ones, not masked ones. There is nowhere for a name or an address to be stored.
  2. A blocklist in the build. tools/check.py fails the build if any ZIP code, street-suffix address pattern, parcel-number pattern, email address, phone number or Social Security number pattern appears anywhere in the site or the data.
  3. Aggregation only. Every published figure is a county, city or metro statistic drawn from a public government release. None of it is derived from the owner's private records.
  4. The calculator transmits nothing. It is a single HTML file with inline JavaScript. There is no network call, no analytics, no font fetch from a third party, and no local storage. Nothing typed into it leaves the browser tab.
  5. No third-party requests at all. No CDN, no analytics, no embedded maps, no tracking pixels. The only outbound links are explicit citations a reader chooses to click.

The one thing this site cannot protect against. A reader who types the address into the calculator is typing it into their own browser, and it still does not leave the tab. But if the owner shares a screenshot of the site with a filled-in calculator, the address leaves with the screenshot. That is a human control, not a technical one, and it is the reason the calculator ships with placeholder values rather than real ones.

Handling of research findings

The brief requires that any personal information encountered during research be treated as internal-only. During this session, research touched only public corporate filings and public government statistics. No individual's personal information was collected, and none is reproduced anywhere.

One deliberate exclusion. The tenant's earnings release names an investor-relations contact with an email address and phone number. That information was read but is deliberately not reproduced on this site; the source is cited by document link instead, so a reader can reach the primary record without this site republishing an individual's contact details.

Where the real records should live

Proposal SG-09 on the suggestions page: a separate, access-logged internal workspace for the actual documents, with this site permanently aggregate-only. The public site should never link to that workspace — a link is itself a disclosure about where the records are.
