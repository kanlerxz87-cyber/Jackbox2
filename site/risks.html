Wealth preservation versus growth

The brief asks for a balance between wealth preservation, opportunity for growth and manageable risk. The appropriate trade-off depends on verified property evidence and the owner's objectives; this page does not rank the paths.

Potential hold considerations

  • Retain ownership and contractual rights under the verified lease.
  • Continue to receive rent if due and paid, subject to costs and lease terms.
  • Maintain exposure to future property value changes, positive or negative.
  • Defer a disposition-related tax event while not selling, subject to advice about other tax consequences.

Potential hold exposures

  • Concentration, tenant credit, building condition and parcel hazards are unknown.
  • Tenant operating outcomes and regulatory costs may affect store viability, but no parcel-specific impact is established.
  • Liquidity and exit pricing may vary with market and property conditions.
  • Remaining term and option mechanics could affect future pricing; the effect must be tested with real offers and lease data.

Decision process. Close the blocking evidence gates, obtain a live market test and a written renewal case, and compare both against the owner's documented objectives and risk limits. This process supplies decision inputs; it does not preselect the outcome.
