A regional statement such as "Los Angeles is seismically active" is true and worthless for pricing — it is recorded below as context and explicitly labelled as not a parcel finding.

Why this is the highest-consequence blank on the site. Hazard status is not a nice-to-have disclosure item. It sets the probable maximum loss, which sets the insurance premium and deductible, which sets the owner's real cash cost of holding. It also determines whether the building can be rebuilt in kind after a loss. An owner can be perfectly right about rents and prices and still be destroyed by a liquefaction finding plus an insurance market that will not write the risk.

Stress design — the three legs together

R-17 requires seismic, flood and fire to be stressed together with loss of rent, repairs and exit liquidity - not as three separate footnotes.

LegDriverNote
Loss of rentScenario months of rent interruption × verified monthly rent; reconcile with lease, tenant performance and insurance.Do not assume rent stops or continues: verify lease obligations, business-interruption coverage and any applicable exclusions.
RepairsVerified repair estimate less applicable insurance recovery, including deductibles, exclusions and code upgrades.Obtain property-specific engineering and insurance evidence; no repair amount is estimated here.
Exit liquidityScenario sale timing and pricing under reduced market activity; do not assign a probability without evidence.No parcel- or submarket-specific post-event transaction dataset was verified for this workspace.

Why the legs must be treated as one event. Run rent interruption, repairs/insurance and exit liquidity jointly as a severe stress scenario. Their degree of correlation and magnitude are not quantified without parcel, policy and market evidence.

The leg owners forget

Exit liquidity. After a regional event, transaction volume in the affected submarket goes to near zero for an extended period. That means the owner cannot sell to escape the other two legs — the usual response to a problem is unavailable at exactly the moment it is needed. Any hold decision should be tested against that scenario before it is made, not after.
