Trade area and neighbourhood

W-01: replacing city and county proxies with genuine trade-area figures. The method, the official instruments, and the honest reason the column is empty.

W-01 status: addressed, gated, not executed. The method for replacing city and county proxies with real trade-area figures is specified below and every official instrument is named. The execution is blocked by one thing: selecting Census tracts requires the parcel location, which is withheld under the privacy rule. Rather than substitute a city number for a trade-area number, this page leaves the trade-area column blank.

Why the distinction matters. County and city averages cannot determine neighborhood conditions or the subject unit's likely demand. Define a local catchment using secure parcel location and documented access, travel, traffic and planning evidence; then select Census tracts under a stated, consistent rule. No radius, population, income or performance estimate is assumed on this page.

Tract selection method

Five steps. Step five matters more than it looks: holding the tract vintage fixed is what makes a trend comparison a trend rather than an artefact of redrawn boundaries.

1Use an authorized, secure workflow to identify the parcel and choose the applicable official parcel/geocoding layer; do not publish its coordinates or APN.Los Angeles County parcel datanot yet
