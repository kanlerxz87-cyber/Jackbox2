Projection discipline

A useful model comes after the evidence, not before it.

Build, validate, then use

Version a dated cash-flow model with explicit observations, assumptions and sources. Use consistent geography and non-overlapping periods where possible. Assemble comparable history before fitting or back-testing; keep a holdout period, assess errors and report limitations. Run base, downside and severe scenarios; do not assign unsupported probabilities or extrapolate city averages to a specific site.
