Irregularities found and how they were handled

#IrregularityHandling
1Two official agencies publish Los Angeles County population estimates that differ by more than 140,000 persons.Both are published with their own source and reference date; neither is averaged. Recorded as F-02.
2The Bureau of Labor Statistics cannot publish an October 2026 12-month CPI change for Los Angeles because October 2025 data were not collected during the federal funding lapse.The index level and the month-on-month change are published; the missing 12-month cell is disclosed rather than filled with the September value.
3No Q4 or full-year FY2026 tenant release appeared in the EDGAR index on the sweep date.The fiscal year is treated as unreported and flagged as F-01; no annual figure is published.
4Census QuickFacts, the state fire marshal's deep map links, the city finance pages, the county recorder and the state Board of Equalization did not respond during the sweep.Those items are blank and listed for manual review with their official URLs. No value was substituted from a secondary source.
5The repository's readme carried unresolved merge-conflict markers on the default branch.Markers removed and the document rewritten to describe the consolidated site; recorded in COORDINATION.md.
6One legacy dashboard's own test suite failed with "table row missing source" and was not run by continuous integration.Recorded as an irregularity, not silenced: the legacy test remains failing and is documented rather than edited, because that directory belongs to another session's scope.
7Six dashboards publish overlapping figures with different cut-off dates.Consolidated here, with the legacy locations kept and dated under Archive. The ledger is the reference.

What was deliberately not done

  • No cap rate, discount rate or sale price was published. Each would require comparable transactions this repository does not hold.
  • No tract-level indicator was published. Publishing one before the parcel's tracts are fixed would be a proxy dressed as evidence.
  • No parcel hazard status was published. It is a privacy question before it is a data question.
  • No forecast was published. A projection without verified inputs is a preference with a chart.

Privacy. No owner name, entity, street address, parcel number, lease file or owner financial record appears on this site. Identifiers are replaced with placeholders and the underlying records stay in an access-controlled internal workspace. Only aggregate, non-identifying findings are published.
