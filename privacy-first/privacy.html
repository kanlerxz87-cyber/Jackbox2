Scope Boundary

What this public site can and cannot do.

In scope (public)

  • Anonymous scenario framing and decision process.
  • Official-source links for area context, hazards, planning, and tax rules.
  • Questions, missing-information checklist, and evidence requirements.
  • Risk register, decision gates, and suggestions for review.
  • Client-side calculator that runs in-browser; no inputs transmitted by this static site.

Out of scope (public)

  • Publishing or inferring parcel address, owner identity, lease terms, or financials.
  • Making a final sell/renew decision without verified inputs.
  • Providing legal, tax, appraisal, engineering, or investment advice.
  • Fabricating market comps, rents, cap rates, taxes, or hazard determinations.

Privacy is top priority. Public output is anonymous by design.
